Brand artwork assets are vendored from Simple Icons (CC0), used to identify the
corresponding products. Brand trademarks remain owned by their respective owners.

- Claude, Telegram, Gmail, Netflix, YouTube: simple-icons 16.0.0.
- OpenAI, Amazon Web Services: simple-icons 13.21.0.
- Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- Download pattern: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@VERSION/icons/NAME.svg

The PNG files are transparent 512px renders of the adjacent SVGs. Rendering and
composition happen locally; product image requests never contact an external CDN.
Product names determine plan and duration labels. Uploaded product photos take
precedence over generated artwork.
